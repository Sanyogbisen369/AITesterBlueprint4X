## Full description (max 4000)
Looking for your next favourite game without spending money? Free Play Picks is a hand-picked list of the best free, top-rated Android games, sorted into the categories players care about.

CATEGORIES
• Combat: shooters, battle royales and action RPGs
• Survival: crafting, base building and zombie survival
• Racing: arcade, drag, drift and kart racing
• Fighting: martial arts, superhero and platform fighters
• In-Person: games to play together in the same room, with local Wi-Fi, pass-and-play, or phones as controllers

FEATURES
• Search by game, studio or feature, like "offline", "co-op" or "local Wi-Fi"
• Sort by top rated, most downloaded or A to Z
• Save games to My List to try later
• Can't decide? Tap "Pick for me" for a random suggestion
• One tap opens the game on Google Play
• Light and dark mode
• No sign-in, no ads, no tracking

Every game listed is free to download. Some include optional in-app purchases. Ratings and download counts are approximate; check each game's store page for current details.

Free Play Picks is an independent guide and is not affiliated with any of the game publishers listed.
